Common questions

Does this connect to my Amazon account?

No — and it never will. Scan & Sort never asks for your delivery-app credentials and does not talk to Amazon’s systems. It reads the printed name off a label with your camera, the same way you read it with your eyes. Your driver account is not involved and is not at risk.

Where is my data stored? Can you see it?

Everything lives on your phone. There is no server, no account, and no copy anywhere else, so nobody — including us — can see your trips. Finished trips delete themselves after 7 days by default. Full detail in the privacy policy.

The camera isn’t picking up the name.

Three things fix almost every case:

Back up a little. Phone cameras can’t focus closer than about four inches. Holding the label too close is the most common cause — aim for roughly an arm’s length, with the name and address block filling the frame.

Turn on the light. Tap the flashlight button in the corner of the camera for dark bins and early mornings.

Hold still for a beat. Let the image sharpen before you tap Capture.

If a label is scuffed, wrinkled, or just stubborn, tap the failed entry in the list to type the name instead — or switch to Type mode and use your keyboard’s microphone to say it.

It read the wrong name.

Tap the entry in the recent list and choose Edit name. On the delivery screen, the pencil button on any row does the same thing. Corrections stick for the whole trip.

I put a package in the wrong zone.

On the loading screen, tap the package in the recent list and pick the right zone — it moves immediately. Already past that point? On the delivery screen, press and hold the package and choose the correct zone.

Someone has two packages in different spots.

The app catches this while you load and offers to move them together. If you keep them apart, that stop is flagged with a ⚠ and lists both zones, so you grab both before walking away.

Can I add a zone in the middle of loading?

Yes. Tap + Zone at the end of the zone strip on the loading screen. Press and hold any zone to rename it. Changes apply to the trip you’re on.

I closed the app mid-route. Did I lose everything?

No. Your trip is saved continuously and survives closing the app, restarting, or a dead battery. Open the app and your active trip is waiting exactly where you left it.

Does it need a signal?

No. Scan & Sort works fully offline — dead-zone warehouses, basement garages, airplane mode. Nothing it does requires a connection.
