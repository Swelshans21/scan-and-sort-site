Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

  • Everything the app records stays on your phone. There is no server and no account.
  • We never collect, transmit, or sell your data — or your customers’.
  • The app records a recipient’s name only. It never stores or transmits street addresses.
  • Label photos are read on the device and deleted immediately. They are never uploaded or saved.
  • Completed trips delete themselves automatically after 7 days.

What this app is

Scan & Sort helps a delivery driver remember which part of their vehicle each package is in. You set up zones for your car, then file each package into one as you load. At each stop, you search a name and the app tells you where to reach.

What is stored, and where

All of the following is written to your phone’s local storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

  • Your vehicles and zones — the names you give them and their order.
  • Your trips — a label and date, and whether the trip is active or finished.
  • Packages — the recipient’s name, whether it’s a box or an envelope, which zone it’s in, and whether it’s been delivered.
  • Your settings — night mode, and how long finished trips are kept.

What is never collected

  • No street addresses. The app captures the recipient’s name and nothing else. To find that name, the camera reads the label’s text on your device — everything other than the name, the address included, is discarded immediately and is never saved, and never leaves your phone.
  • No account. No email, no password, no sign-in.
  • No location. The app never requests or uses GPS.
  • No contacts, photo library, or microphone.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party tracking or analytics.

Camera

The app asks for camera access for one purpose: reading the printed recipient name off a shipping label. When you tap Capture Name, the app takes a single photo, reads the text on it on your device, and then deletes the photo — including when the read fails. The image is never uploaded, never added to your photo library, and never kept.

Automatic deletion

Recipient names are other people’s personal information, so the app does not hold onto them. Once a trip is marked complete, it and all of its packages are permanently deleted from your phone after 7 days. You can also delete any trip yourself at any time.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a diagnostic report so the problem can be fixed. These reports contain technical information only — the type of error, where in the code it happened, the device model, and the app version.

They are automatically filtered before sending to strip anything resembling personal information, and the app is built never to place a recipient’s name into an error message in the first place. Crash reports contain no names, no addresses, no account information, and no advertising identifiers. They are processed by Sentry, a crash-reporting service.

Your control over your data

  • Delete any trip, package, or vehicle from inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes everything. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, uninstalling permanently erases all of it. There is no copy for us to hold, hand over, or lose.

A note for drivers

The names in this app belong to the people you deliver to. Treat that list the way you would want yours treated: keep your phone locked, and let finished trips age out rather than keeping them forever.

Children

Scan & Sort is a tool for working delivery drivers. It is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top will be updated and the change will be described in the app’s release notes. Meaningful changes will never be applied retroactively to data already on your phone.
